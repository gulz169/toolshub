About this tool

Crop any photo into a perfect circle and download it as a transparent PNG. Ideal for profile pictures on WhatsApp, Instagram and websites.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the downloaded file PNG?

PNG supports transparency, so the corners outside the circle stay see-through instead of turning white or black.

Can I move the picture inside the circle?

Yes. Drag and zoom the photo so the face or subject sits where you want it.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The cropping happens in your browser and the photo stays on your device.
