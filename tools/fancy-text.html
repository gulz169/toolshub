Frequently asked questions

Why can these fonts be pasted anywhere?

They are special Unicode characters, not real fonts, so they work in any app that supports Unicode.

Does it work with Urdu?

No. These styles are made for English letters and numbers only.

Why do some styles show boxes?

Some older devices or apps lack the symbols. Try another style if boxes appear.
