Frequently asked questions

How is the average calculated?

The average is the sum of all numbers divided by how many numbers there are.

How do I separate the numbers?

Use commas, spaces or new lines. Anything that is not a number is ignored.

What is the difference between mean and median?

The mean is the usual average. The median is the middle value when numbers are sorted, and it is less affected by very large or very small values.
