Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin is profit as a percent of the selling price. Markup is profit as a percent of the cost.

How do I set a selling price for a target margin?

Enter your cost and the margin you want, and the tool gives the price you need to charge.

Does it include taxes and shipping?

Add extra costs such as shipping or fees into your cost for a realistic profit figure.
