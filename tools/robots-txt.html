Frequently asked questions

Where do I put robots.txt?

Upload it to the root of your site so it opens at yoursite.com/robots.txt.

Does robots.txt hide pages from Google?

Not completely. It asks crawlers not to visit, but a blocked page can still appear if linked elsewhere. Use noindex to keep pages out of results.

Should I add my sitemap?

Yes. A sitemap line helps search engines find your pages.
