Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI?

For adults, the WHO range for normal weight is 18.5 to 24.9. Below 18.5 is underweight, 25 to 29.9 is overweight and 30 or more is obese.

Is BMI always accurate?

No. It does not separate muscle from fat, so athletes can show a high BMI. It is a rough guide, not a diagnosis.

Can I use this for children?

This tool uses adult ranges. Children and teens need age-based growth charts, so ask a doctor.
