Frequently asked questions

What is a UUID?

A UUID is a 128-bit unique identifier, written like 123e4567-e89b-12d3-a456-426614174000.

Can two UUIDs be the same?

It is extremely unlikely. Random UUIDs are designed to be practically unique.

Is it safe for secrets?

UUIDs are identifiers, not passwords. Use a password generator for secrets.
