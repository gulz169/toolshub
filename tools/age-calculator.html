About this tool

Find your exact age in years, months and days from your date of birth, and see how many days are left until your next birthday. Useful for forms, job applications and school admissions.

Frequently asked questions

How is my age calculated?

The tool counts full years, then remaining months and days, between your date of birth and the date you choose (today by default).

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the 'age at' date to see how old you were or will be on any day, for example a job deadline or admission cut-off date.

Is my date of birth saved anywhere?

No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
