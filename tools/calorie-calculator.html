Frequently asked questions

What is BMR?

Basal Metabolic Rate is the energy your body burns at complete rest. Your daily needs are BMR multiplied by an activity factor.

How many calories should I cut to lose weight?

A moderate deficit of about 300 to 500 calories below maintenance is a common starting point. Avoid very low-calorie diets without medical advice.

Are the results exact?

No. They are estimates from a standard formula. Real needs vary with body type, health and daily movement.
