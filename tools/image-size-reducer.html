How to use

  1. Add a photo.
  2. Enter the size you need in KB, or tap a preset.
  3. Press Compress, then download.

Very small targets may lower the image size (pixels) and quality. Your photo never leaves your device.

About this tool

Compress an image to a target size in KB, such as 50KB or 100KB, for government forms, job portals and online applications.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get an image under 100KB?

Enter 100 as the target size and the tool lowers quality and size until the file fits.

Will the photo still be clear?

Very small targets reduce quality. If it looks too soft, try a slightly larger target.

Is my image uploaded?

No. It is processed on your device.
