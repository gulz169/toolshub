About this tool

Encode text so it is safe to use in a URL, or decode percent-encoded links back to readable text.

Frequently asked questions

Why do URLs need encoding?

Spaces and special characters are not allowed in URLs, so they are replaced with %-codes.

Does it work with Urdu in links?

Yes. Urdu text is encoded as UTF-8 percent codes and can be decoded back.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. It is processed in your browser.
