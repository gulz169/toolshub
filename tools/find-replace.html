How to use

Paste text, enter what to find and its replacement. Turn on regex if you need patterns.

Nothing you enter leaves your device.

About this tool

Find and replace words or phrases across your whole text at once. Supports case matching and works with Urdu and English.

Frequently asked questions

Does it replace every match?

Yes. All matches in the text are replaced in one go, and you can see the count.

Is the search case-sensitive?

You can turn case matching on or off depending on whether 'Word' and 'word' should be treated as different.

Can I undo a replace?

Keep a copy of your original text. If a replace is wrong, paste the original back and try again.
