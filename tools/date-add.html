How to use

Pick a start date and enter how much to add (or a negative number to subtract).

Nothing you enter leaves your device.

About this tool

Add or subtract days, weeks, months or years from any date and see the exact result with its weekday. Useful for due dates, deadlines and notice periods.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find a date 90 days from today?

Choose today's date, enter 90 days and pick Add. The result shows the new date and its weekday.

How are months handled?

Adding a month keeps the same day number where possible. If that day does not exist, such as 31 February, the date moves to the last day of the month.

Can I subtract days?

Yes. Choose Subtract to go back in time, for example to find a start date.
