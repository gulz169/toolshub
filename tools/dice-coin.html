About this tool

Roll one or more dice or flip a coin online for games, decisions and classroom fun. Every result is random.

Frequently asked questions

Is the result really random?

It uses your browser's random number generator, which is fine for games and everyday decisions.

Can I roll more than one die?

Yes. Choose how many dice to roll and the total is shown.

Is a coin flip fair?

Yes. Heads and tails each have an equal chance.
