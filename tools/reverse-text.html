About this tool

Reverse text by characters, words or lines in one click. Works with English and Urdu.

Frequently asked questions

What can I reverse?

You can reverse the whole text, the order of words or the order of lines.

Does it work with Urdu?

Yes, though reversing Urdu letters can break the joined script. Reversing word or line order works better.

Is my text saved?

No. It stays in your browser.
