Frequently asked questions

How much can an image be compressed?

It depends on the photo and quality level. Photos often shrink a lot with little visible loss at medium quality.

Will quality be lost?

Some detail is removed to save space. Use a higher quality setting if you need sharper results.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Compression is done on your device.
