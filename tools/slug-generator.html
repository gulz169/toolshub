Frequently asked questions

What is a slug?

A slug is the readable part of a URL, such as my-first-post in example.com/my-first-post.

Why use hyphens?

Search engines read hyphens as word separators, so they are better than underscores or spaces.

How long should a slug be?

Keep it short and meaningful, usually 3 to 6 words.
