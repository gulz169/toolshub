Frequently asked questions

Why resize to A4?

A4 is the standard paper size in Pakistan and most countries, so printing is easier and cleaner.

Will my content be cut off?

Pages are scaled to fit, so content should stay inside the page. Check the result before printing.

Is my PDF uploaded?
