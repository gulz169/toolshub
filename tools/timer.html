    How to use

    1. Choose Stopwatch or Countdown.
    2. Press Start. Use Lap to record split times.
    3. The countdown plays a sound when time is up.

    Keep this tab open while the countdown runs. Your browser may slow timers in background tabs.

    About this tool

    Use a stopwatch or a countdown timer in your browser, for study sessions, cooking, workouts and more.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does the timer keep running in the background?

    It is best to keep the page open. Mobile browsers may pause pages that are in the background.

    Can I use laps?

    Yes. The stopwatch lets you record laps while it keeps running.

    Will it make a sound when time is up?

    Make sure your device volume is on. Browsers may block sound until you have interacted with the page.
