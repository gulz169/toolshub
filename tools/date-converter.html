How to use

  1. Pick an English date to see the Hijri date, or enter a Hijri date.
  2. In Pakistan the moon is sighted locally, so use the Adjust box to match your calendar.

Dates are calculated, not sighted. Islamic months can differ by 1-2 days from the announced date.

About this tool

Convert dates between the Hijri (Islamic) and Gregorian calendars. Results come from your browser's built-in calendar data.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Hijri date always exact?

Not always. It is a calculated date, and actual Islamic months start with moon sighting, so it can differ by a day from your local announcement.

Which Hijri calendar is used?

The tool uses the Islamic calendar variants provided by your browser. Pick the one you prefer from the options.

Can I use it for Ramadan or Eid dates?

Yes for a good estimate, but always confirm the final date with your local moon-sighting committee.
