How to use

  1. Add a PDF.
  2. Draw or upload your signature, then click the page where it should go.
  3. Download the signed PDF.

This adds a picture of your signature. It is not a legal digital certificate. Your files never leave your device.

About this tool

Add your handwritten-style signature to a PDF. Draw or upload it, place it on the page and download the signed file.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a legally binding digital signature?

It adds an image of your signature, which is fine for many everyday forms. Some contracts need certified digital signatures, so check the requirement.

Can I move and resize the signature?

Yes. Place it on the right page and adjust its position and size.

Is my PDF or signature uploaded?

No. Both stay in your browser.
