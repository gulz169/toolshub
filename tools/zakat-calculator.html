How to use

  1. Enter today's gold and silver price per gram.
  2. Fill in your assets and debts.
  3. Zakat is due if your net wealth reaches the nisab and a full lunar year has passed.

Nisab: 52.5 tola silver (612.36 g) or 7.5 tola gold (87.48 g). Scholars differ on details, so confirm with a trusted scholar.
