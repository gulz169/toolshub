How to use

  1. Enter today's gold and silver price per gram.
  2. Fill in your assets and debts.
  3. Zakat is due if your net wealth reaches the nisab and a full lunar year has passed.

Nisab: 52.5 tola silver (612.36 g) or 7.5 tola gold (87.48 g). Scholars differ on details, so confirm with a trusted scholar.

About this tool

Calculate Zakat at 2.5% on your cash, gold, silver, business assets and receivables, after subtracting debts, and check the nisab.

Frequently asked questions

What is the nisab?

This calculator uses 87.48 g of gold or 612.36 g of silver. Enter the current price to find the nisab amount.

Which nisab should I choose, gold or silver?

Scholars differ. Silver gives a lower nisab, so many people choose it for caution. Ask a scholar you trust.

Is this a religious ruling?

No. It is a calculation aid. For your exact situation, ask a qualified scholar.
