Frequently asked questions

How is hourly pay worked out from monthly salary?

The yearly salary is divided by total working hours in a year, using your hours per day and days per week.

Can I change working hours?

Yes. Set your hours per day and days per week for a more accurate result.

Does it include tax?

No. It converts the amount you enter. Use gross or net salary as you prefer.
