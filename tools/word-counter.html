About this tool

Count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs as you type, and see the estimated reading time. Works with English and Urdu.

Frequently asked questions

Does it count Urdu words?

Yes. Words are counted by spaces, so Urdu text is counted properly.

Are spaces counted as characters?

Both counts are shown: characters with spaces and characters without spaces.

Is my text saved?

No. It stays in your browser and is never uploaded.
