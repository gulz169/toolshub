How to use

Add an image, choose rows and columns, and download all pieces as a ZIP.

Nothing you enter leaves your device.

About this tool

Split an image into a grid of equal pieces, for example 3×3 for an Instagram grid or tiles for printing.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make an Instagram grid?

Choose a 3×3 or 3×1 grid, split the image, then upload the pieces in reverse order.

Can I download all pieces together?

Yes. You can download the pieces and use them separately.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Splitting is done in your browser.
