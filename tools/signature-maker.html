Draw your signature or type it in a handwriting style and download it as a transparent PNG.

Frequently asked questions

Why a transparent PNG?

A transparent background lets you place the signature on documents without a white box.

Can I draw with my finger?

Yes. On a phone you can draw with your finger, and on a computer with the mouse.

Is my signature saved online?

No. It is created in your browser and not stored on a server.
