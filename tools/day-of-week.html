Frequently asked questions

Which day was I born on?

Enter your date of birth and the tool shows the weekday you were born.

Does it work for very old dates?

Yes, it uses the standard Gregorian calendar for any year you enter.

Can it show the day for a Hijri date?

Use the Hijri Date Converter first to get the Gregorian date, then check the weekday here.
