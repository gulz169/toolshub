How to use

  1. Enter a price and discount % to see the sale price.
  2. For tax, pick whether to add it or take it out of the amount.

Nothing you enter leaves your device.

About this tool

Work out the discounted price and add or remove GST or sales tax. Useful for shopping, invoices and small business pricing.

Frequently asked questions

How is the discount calculated?

The discount amount is price multiplied by discount percent divided by 100, and it is subtracted from the original price.

Can I remove GST from a price that already includes it?

Yes. Choose the option for tax-inclusive prices and the tool shows the base price and the tax separately.

Which GST rate should I enter?

Enter the rate that applies to your product or country. The tool does not assume one.
