About this tool

Click anywhere on an image to get the exact color as HEX and RGB. Useful for matching colors in a design or a logo.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a color from my photo?

Upload the image and click the spot you want. The HEX and RGB values appear and can be copied.

Why do colors look slightly different?

Screens, compression and lighting in the photo affect colors. The tool reads the pixel exactly as it appears in the image.

Is my image uploaded?

No. It is read in your browser only.
