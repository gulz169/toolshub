How to use

Type a pattern, choose flags, and paste your text. Matches are highlighted. Add a replacement to preview it.

Nothing you enter leaves your device.

About this tool

Test regular expressions against sample text and see the matches highlighted live. Supports common flags such as g, i and m.

Frequently asked questions

Which regex flavour is used?

It uses your browser's JavaScript regular expression engine.

What do the flags mean?

g finds all matches, i ignores case and m lets ^ and $ match at each line.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. Testing runs in your browser.
