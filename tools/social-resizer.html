Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between fill and fit?

Fill crops the image to cover the whole size. Fit keeps the whole image and adds bars.

Which size is a YouTube thumbnail?

1280×720 pixels, which is a 16:9 ratio.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Resizing is done in your browser.
