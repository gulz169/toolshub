How to use

  1. Add a front-facing photo.
  2. Pick the size, then use Zoom and the sliders to place the face.
  3. Download one photo or a print sheet with copies.

Sizes are made at 300 DPI. Always check the exact rules of the office that asks for the photo. Your files never leave your device.
