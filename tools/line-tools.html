How to use

  1. Paste your list, one item per line.
  2. Press any button. You can press several in a row.

Your text never leaves your device.

About this tool

Sort lines, remove duplicate lines, reverse order, add line numbers and shuffle a list in a few clicks.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove duplicate lines?

Paste your list and choose the remove-duplicates option. Each repeated line is kept once.

Does sorting work for Urdu?

Yes, it sorts any text your browser supports.

Can I use it on a big list?

Yes, but very long lists may be slower on old phones.
