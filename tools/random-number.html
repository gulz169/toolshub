Frequently asked questions

Can I avoid repeated numbers?

Yes. Turn on the unique option to get numbers without repeats, like a lottery draw.

Is it truly random?

It uses your browser's random generator, which is fine for games and draws but not for security keys.

Can I use it for a giveaway?

Yes, for informal giveaways. Record the draw so participants can trust it.
