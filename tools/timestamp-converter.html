Frequently asked questions

What is a Unix timestamp?

It is the number of seconds since 1 January 1970 UTC.

How do I know if it is seconds or milliseconds?

A 10-digit number is usually seconds and a 13-digit number is usually milliseconds.

Which time zone is shown?

You can view both UTC and your local time zone.
