Frequently asked questions

What are the common number bases?

Binary is base 2, octal is base 8, decimal is base 10 and hexadecimal is base 16.

Why does hex use letters?

Hex needs 16 symbols, so after 0 to 9 it uses A to F for 10 to 15.

Can I convert negative or very large numbers?

Use whole numbers. Very large values may lose precision depending on the browser.
