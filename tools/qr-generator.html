Frequently asked questions

Does the QR code expire?

No. These QR codes contain your data directly, so they do not expire. If the link inside changes, update the QR code.

Can I print it?

Yes. Download a large image and print it with good contrast. Test scan before printing many copies.

Is my data sent to a server?

No. The code is created in your browser.
