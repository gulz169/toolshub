Frequently asked questions

How do I use the generated code?

Copy the box-shadow line and paste it into the CSS rule of any element, such as a card or button.

What does spread do?

Spread makes the shadow bigger or smaller than the element. Positive values grow it and negative values shrink it.

Can I make an inner shadow?

Yes. Turn on the inset option to place the shadow inside the box.
