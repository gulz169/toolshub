Frequently asked questions

How is GPA calculated?

Each grade becomes grade points, multiplied by the course's credit hours. The total is divided by the total credit hours.

What is the difference between GPA and CGPA?

GPA is for one semester. CGPA combines all your semesters.

Will it match my university?

Universities use slightly different grade scales. Check that the grade points match your university's policy.
