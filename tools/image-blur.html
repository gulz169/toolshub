Frequently asked questions

Is blurring enough to hide text?

Light blur can sometimes be reversed. For sensitive info like CNIC or card numbers, use strong blur or pixelation, or cover it fully.

Can I blur only one area?

Yes. Select the area you want and apply the effect only there.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. The effect is applied in your browser.
