Frequently asked questions

What is an aspect ratio?

It is the proportional relationship between width and height, written like 16:9 or 4:3. Screens, photos and videos all use standard ratios.

How do I resize without stretching?

Enter the original size and one new dimension. The tool gives the other dimension so the picture keeps its shape.

Which ratio is best for YouTube or Instagram?

YouTube videos use 16:9. Instagram posts commonly use 1:1 or 4:5, and Stories and Reels use 9:16.
