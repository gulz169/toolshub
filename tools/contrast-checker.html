Frequently asked questions

What contrast ratio do I need?

WCAG AA asks for at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. AAA asks for 7:1 and 4.5:1.

Why does contrast matter?

Low contrast is hard to read for people with weak eyesight and in bright sunlight, and it can hurt your site's usability.

How can I fix a failing pair?

Make the text darker or the background lighter (or the reverse) until the ratio passes.
