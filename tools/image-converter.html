Frequently asked questions

Which format should I choose?

JPG is best for photos, PNG for graphics and transparency, and WebP for small files on modern websites.

Will resizing stretch my image?

Keep the aspect ratio option on to avoid stretching when you change width or height.

Is my image uploaded?

No. Conversion runs in your browser.
