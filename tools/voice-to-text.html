How to use

  1. Choose the language you will speak.
  2. Press Start and allow the microphone.
  3. Speak. Press Stop when done.

Works in Chrome and Edge. These browsers send your voice to their speech service online, so do not use it for private content.

About this tool

Speak into your microphone and see your words written as text. Supports Urdu and English where your browser allows.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the microphone not working?

Allow microphone access when your browser asks. Chrome on Android and desktop usually works best.

Is my voice uploaded?

Speech recognition is provided by your browser, which may process audio using its own speech service. This site does not store your voice.

How can I get better accuracy?

Speak clearly in a quiet place and choose the correct language before you start.
