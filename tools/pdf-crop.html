Frequently asked questions

Can I crop all pages at once?

Yes. Set the margin amounts and apply them to the whole document.

Is content deleted permanently?

Cropping changes the visible page area. Keep your original file in case you need the full page later.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
