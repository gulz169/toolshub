How to use

  1. Add a PDF.
  2. Drag pages, or use the ◀ ▶ buttons, to change the order.
  3. Download the new PDF.

Your files never leave your device.

About this tool

Reorder the pages of a PDF by dragging them into the sequence you want, then save the new file.

Frequently asked questions

How do I move a page?

Drag a page thumbnail to a new position, then save the PDF.

Can I delete pages here?

Use the Rotate & Delete Pages tool for removing pages. This tool focuses on changing the order.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
