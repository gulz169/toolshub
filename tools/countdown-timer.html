About this tool

Count down to any date and time, such as an exam, wedding, Eid or trip, and see the days, hours, minutes and seconds left.

Frequently asked questions

Does the countdown keep running if I close the page?

The display updates only while the page is open. Reopen it and it recalculates from the current time.

Which time zone is used?

Your device's local time zone.

Can I count down to a past date?

No, the target must be in the future. For past dates, use the Date Calculator to see how many days have passed.
