How to use

Add a PDF and download the grayscale version. Pages become images, so text is no longer selectable.

Nothing you enter leaves your device.

About this tool

Convert a colored PDF to black and white to save ink when printing and reduce file size.

Frequently asked questions

Why convert a PDF to black and white?

It saves color ink when printing and can make the file smaller.

Will the text stay selectable?

Pages are rebuilt as images, so text may not be selectable afterwards.

Is my file uploaded?

No. Conversion runs in your browser.
