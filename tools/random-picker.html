How to use

  1. Type the names, one per line.
  2. Press Spin. The winner is chosen fairly at random.

Everything runs in your browser.

About this tool

Paste a list of names or options and let the tool pick one at random. Great for giveaways, classrooms and decisions.

Frequently asked questions

How do I enter the list?

Type one name or option per line, then press the pick button.

Can I pick more than one winner?

Yes. Choose how many winners you want if the option is available, or run it again.

Is the pick fair?

Every item has the same chance of being picked.
