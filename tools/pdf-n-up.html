About this tool

Place 2, 4 or more PDF pages on one sheet to save paper when printing handouts and notes.

Frequently asked questions

What does N-up mean?

N-up puts N pages onto one sheet, such as 2-up or 4-up, to use less paper.

Will the text be too small?

More pages per sheet means smaller text. 2-up is usually easy to read, while 6-up or more may be tiny.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. It is processed in your browser.
