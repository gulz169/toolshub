Frequently asked questions

What is a favicon?

It is the small icon shown in the browser tab, bookmarks and search results next to your site name.

Which sizes do I need?

A 32×32 icon for browsers and a 180×180 Apple touch icon cover most sites. Larger 192 and 512 sizes help with app installs.

How do I add it to my site?

Upload the files and link them in the head of your HTML with link rel='icon' tags.
