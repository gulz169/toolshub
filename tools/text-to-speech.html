How to use

  1. Type or paste text.
  2. Pick a voice that matches the language of the text.
  3. Press Speak.

Voices come from your device. If there is no Urdu voice, install one in your system or browser settings.

About this tool

Listen to any text read aloud using voices on your device, including Urdu if your device has an Urdu voice.

Frequently asked questions

Why is there no Urdu voice?

Voices come from your device or browser. If none is installed, only other languages appear. Installing an Urdu voice in your device settings can help.

Can I change speed?

Yes. Adjust the rate and pitch to your liking.

Can I download the audio?

The tool speaks using your device voices. It does not create a downloadable audio file.
