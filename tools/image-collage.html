Frequently asked questions

How many photos can I add?

You can add several photos, depending on the layout you pick.

Can I change the spacing and background?

Yes. You can adjust the gap between photos and choose a background color.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The collage is made in your browser and your photos stay on your device.
