About this tool

Break a URL into protocol, domain, path, query parameters and hash, so you can read and debug links easily.

Frequently asked questions

What parts of a URL are shown?

Protocol, host, port, path, query parameters and hash fragment.

What are query parameters?

They are the key=value pairs after the ? in a URL, such as ?id=5&page=2.

Is my URL sent anywhere?

No. Parsing runs in your browser.
