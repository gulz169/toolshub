Frequently asked questions

Does it support lakh and crore?

Yes. You can choose the lakh/crore style that is common in Pakistan and India.

Can I use it for cheques?

Yes, it helps you write the amount in words. Always double-check before writing on a legal document.

Does it work with decimals?

It is designed for whole numbers. For amounts with paisa, convert the rupee and paisa parts separately.
