How to use

  1. Add your PDF.
  2. Press ↻ to rotate a page, ✕ to remove it.
  3. Download the new PDF.

Your files never leave your device.

About this tool

Rotate pages or delete unwanted pages from a PDF, then download the cleaned file.

Frequently asked questions

How do I rotate one page only?

Select the page and rotate it. Other pages stay unchanged.

Can I delete several pages?

Yes. Mark the pages you do not need and remove them.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser.
