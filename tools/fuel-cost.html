About this tool

Estimate the fuel cost of a trip from distance, mileage and petrol or diesel price. Plan your travel budget before you leave.

Frequently asked questions

How is fuel cost calculated?

Fuel needed = distance ÷ mileage. Cost = fuel needed × price per litre.

What mileage should I enter?

Use your car's real average in km per litre. City driving and AC use usually give lower mileage than the showroom figure.

Can I calculate a round trip?

Yes. Enter the total distance for going and returning.
