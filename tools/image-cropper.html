How to use

  1. Add a photo and pick a ratio, or keep it free.
  2. Drag inside the box to move it, drag the corners to resize.
  3. Press Crop & download. The cut keeps the original quality.

Your image never leaves your device.

About this tool

Crop a photo to any size or common ratio such as square, 4:3 and 16:9, then download it. Simple and fast on phone or computer.

Frequently asked questions

Can I crop to a fixed ratio?

Yes. Pick a ratio such as 1:1 or 16:9 and drag the crop box.

Does cropping reduce quality?

No extra compression is added beyond saving the cropped area, but a smaller crop means fewer pixels.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Cropping happens in your browser.
