Frequently asked questions

How do I find the time in another country?

Choose your city and the other city, enter the time and the converted time is shown.

Does it handle daylight saving time?

Yes. It uses your browser's time zone data, which includes daylight saving rules.

What is Pakistan's time zone?

Pakistan Standard Time (PKT) is UTC+5.
