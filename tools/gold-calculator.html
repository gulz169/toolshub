Frequently asked questions

How many grams is one tola?

This calculator uses 1 tola = 11.664 grams. One tola is 12 masha and one masha is 8 ratti.

Does it show today's gold rate?

No. Enter the current price per tola from your local jeweller or market, and the tool calculates the value.

What do 24K and 22K mean?

They show gold purity. 24K is pure gold, and 22K is about 91.6% gold mixed with other metals.
