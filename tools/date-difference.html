About this tool

Find the exact number of days, weeks, months and years between two dates. Good for service length, contracts and event planning.

Frequently asked questions

Are both dates counted?

By default the difference is the gap between the two dates. Turn on the include-end-date option if you want to count both the first and last day.

Can the start date be after the end date?

Yes. The tool shows the difference regardless of order.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes. Leap years are counted correctly.
