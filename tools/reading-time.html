Frequently asked questions

How is reading time estimated?

It divides the word count by an average reading speed, usually about 200 to 250 words per minute.

Is speaking time different?

Yes. People speak slower than they read, around 130 to 150 words per minute.

Does it work for Urdu?

Yes, it counts words in any language, though reading speed varies between people.
