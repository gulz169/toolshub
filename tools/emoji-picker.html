Frequently asked questions

How do I copy an emoji?

Tap or click an emoji and it is copied to your clipboard. Then paste it where you need it.

Why does an emoji look different on another phone?

Each platform draws emojis in its own style, so the same emoji can look slightly different on Android, iPhone and Windows.

Can I search by name?

Yes. Type a word like 'heart' or 'smile' in the search box.
