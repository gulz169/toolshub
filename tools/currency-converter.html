Frequently asked questions

Where do the exchange rates come from?

They come from a public daily rates service and are cached for about six hours. They are for reference, not the exact rate a bank or money changer will give.

Do I need internet?

Yes, at least once to fetch the latest rates. After that the saved rates can still be used for a few hours.

Why is the open-market rate different?

Open-market and bank rates include buying and selling margins and local demand, so they can differ from the mid-market rate shown here.
