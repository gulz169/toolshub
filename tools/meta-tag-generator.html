Frequently asked questions

How long should a title and description be?

Keep titles around 50 to 60 characters and descriptions around 150 to 160 so search results do not cut them off.

What are Open Graph tags?

They control the title, description and image shown when your link is shared on Facebook, WhatsApp and similar apps.

Do meta tags guarantee rankings?

No, but good titles and descriptions help people click your result.
