How to use

  1. Choose your PDF.
  2. Type the pages you want (like 1-3, 5) or choose to split every page.
  3. Press Split and download the result.

Your file never leaves your device.

About this tool

Split a PDF into separate files or extract only the pages you need, such as 1-3 or 5, 8, 10.

Frequently asked questions

How do I extract specific pages?

Type the page numbers or ranges, for example 1-3, 5, 8, and extract them into a new PDF.

Does the original file change?

No. Your original stays as it is and you download new files.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Splitting happens in your browser.
