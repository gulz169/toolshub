Frequently asked questions

How is electricity cost calculated?

Units (kWh) = watts × hours ÷ 1000. Cost = units × your price per unit.

Where do I find an appliance's wattage?

Look at the label on the appliance or its manual. AC and heater ratings are usually printed in watts.

Why does my real bill differ?

Bills include slab rates, taxes and fuel adjustments, and real use varies. Treat this as a close estimate.
