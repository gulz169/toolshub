Click to choose images or drag & drop here JPG, PNG or WebP. Clear, straight photos work best.

How to use

  1. Add one or more images.
  2. Choose the language of the text.
  3. Press Extract text, then copy or download.

The first run downloads the language data (a few MB), so it takes longer. Always check the result for mistakes, especially Urdu.

Your images are processed on your device and never uploaded.
