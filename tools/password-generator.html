Frequently asked questions

How long should a password be?

At least 12 to 16 characters with letters, numbers and symbols is a good start. Longer is stronger.

Is the password saved or sent anywhere?

No. It is created in your browser and disappears when you close the page.

Should I reuse passwords?

No. Use a different password for every account and keep them in a password manager.
