Frequently asked questions

How is WPM calculated?

Words per minute counts correctly typed characters divided by 5, per minute of typing.

What is a good typing speed?

Around 40 WPM is average, and 60 or more is considered good for office work.

How can I improve?

Practice regularly, look at the screen instead of the keys and focus on accuracy first.
