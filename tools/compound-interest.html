Frequently asked questions

What is compound interest?

It is interest earned on both your original money and the interest already added, so growth speeds up over time.

Does more frequent compounding matter?

Yes, a little. Monthly compounding earns slightly more than yearly at the same rate.

Is this suitable for Islamic savings?

The tool only does the math. If you avoid interest, use it just to understand how growth works and choose a Shariah-compliant product.
