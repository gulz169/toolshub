About this tool

Add page numbers to a PDF in the position you choose, with options for start number and size.

Frequently asked questions

Can I choose where the numbers go?

Yes. Pick a position such as bottom center or bottom right.

Can numbering start from a different number?

Yes. Set the start number if your document begins after a cover or contents page.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. It is processed in your browser.
