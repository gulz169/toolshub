About this tool

Find speed, distance or time when you know the other two. Handy for travel planning and school problems.

Frequently asked questions

What is the formula?

Speed = distance ÷ time. Distance = speed × time. Time = distance ÷ speed.

Which units can I use?

Use consistent units, such as km and hours for km/h, or choose the units offered in the tool.

Can I plan a road trip with it?

Yes. Enter the distance and your average speed to estimate travel time. Add time for breaks and traffic.
